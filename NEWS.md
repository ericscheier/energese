# odumesl 0.1.0

Initial CRAN release.

* First R grammar-of-graphics library for H.T. Odum's Energy Systems
  Language (ESL).
* Seven `geom_odum_*` ggproto Geoms (source, producer, consumer,
  storage, interaction, heat sink, money).
* Seven low-level `odum_*` vertex helpers returning tibbles of
  polygon coordinates for direct use with `geom_polygon()` or
  non-ggplot renderers.
* Vignette + prior-art scan.
